Budget comparison

Three equipment levels.

Baseline<$10k

Uses existing equipment where possible and keeps the handheld camera close to production.

  • Lowest equipment cost
  • More separate boxes and cables
  • Limited backup options
Standard~$20k

Adds a dedicated production workflow and a more practical connection for the handheld camera.

  • PTZ cameras with built-in SRT
  • Mobile handheld camera

Redundant~$45k

  • Redundant network components
  • ISO recording and bonded video
  • Additional power, audio and comms

10 / Gear detail

Gear detail · transmission

Fixed RF and handheld links.

01

Fixed PTZ network

Use directional CPE radios from each PTZ to an elevated RF hub. Four feeds at 12–16 Mbps each total about 48–64 Mbps.

PTZ → Ethernet → directional CPE → elevated RF hub
  • Ubiquiti airMAX at the lower two budgets
  • Dedicated point-to-multipoint network at ~$45k
  • Prioritize line of sight, interference checks and antenna placement
  • Include sectors, weatherproof enclosures, PoE, spare radios and redundant backhaul as budget allows
02

Roaming handheld

A walking operator should not use the directional radio setup used by the fixed PTZ cameras.

  • Under $10k: limited-range conventional wireless video or SRT
  • ~$20k: Magewell Ultra Encode SDI over the selected mobile RF or cellular network
  • ~$45k: LiveU Solo Pro with bonded cellular
03

Magewell Ultra Encode SDI

1080p60, H.264/H.265, SRT Caller/Listener and up to 16 Mbps.

04

LiveU Solo Pro

SDI + HDMI, H.264/H.265, 1080p60, 4K60, internal battery and LRT transport. Supports up to six bonded links, four external cellular modems, Wi-Fi and Ethernet.

13 / Gear detail

Gear detail · production

Switching, decoding and graphics.

Switcher inputs

The eight-input layout is:

  1. PTZ 1
  2. PTZ 2
  3. PTZ 3
  4. PTZ 4
  5. Handheld
  6. Drone
  7. Graphics
  8. Spare

Switcher by budget

  • Under $10k: ATEM SDI Extreme ISO
  • ~$20k: ATEM Television Studio HD8
  • ~$45k: ATEM Television Studio HD8 ISO

The HD8 ISO was listed at $4,395. It records all eight inputs, program, audio and a Resolve project, and includes direct RTMP streaming. The noted premium over HD8 is about $1,100.

Decoding

Five separate SRT decoders will work, but add power supplies, Ethernet, SDI cables, setup and failure points. At the two higher budgets, evaluate a multi-channel decoder or a vMix receive computer with a multi-output SDI card.

5 SRT feeds → 5 SDI outputs → ATEM

Graphics and replay

Use a dedicated vMix computer: HD at under $10k, 4K at ~$20k and Pro at ~$45k.

Possible inputs: timing data · rider database · lap counter · leaderboard · GPS · heart rate · instant replay · sponsor graphics

14 / Gear detail

Gear detail · crew systems

Audio, comms and the working build.

Audio requirements

Plan for commentary, ambient course sound, the start/finish PA feed and possibly natural sound from remote cameras.

  • 2 commentator headsets or microphones
  • Small digital audio mixer
  • Ambient stereo or shotgun microphones
  • PA feed isolation / DI
  • Audio monitoring

Send the completed audio mix to the ATEM.

Comms requirements

Full-duplex communication is needed between the director, PTZ operator, handheld operator and drone pilot.

  • Basic system at the lower budget
  • Hollyland or Saramonic at ~$20k
  • Higher-end wireless intercom at ~$45k
  • Consider ATEM talkback integration

The source notes a basic example at about $489 but does not name the model.

Detailed ~$20k working build

  • 4× PTZOptics Move 4K 20× using internal SRT over a private fixed RF network
  • 1× existing handheld with Magewell SRT encoder over mobile RF/cellular
  • 1× existing drone, controller HDMI converted to SDI
  • ATEM Television Studio HD8 and PTZ joystick
  • Dedicated vMix graphics PC
  • Audio mixer, commentary and wireless intercom
  • Peplink, Starlink and two cellular carriers
  • Entire race coverage workflow at 1080p59.94
